# Seller fee tier schedule

Fee is applied to the gross order amount to determine the net payout. Tier changes take effect on the date a seller crosses a threshold, not at the start of the next billing period.

| Trailing 4-week GMV | Fee rate |
| --- | --- |
| Under $5,000 | 12% |
| $5,000 to $20,000 | 9% |
| Over $20,000 | 7% |

- Copperline Trading: 9%
